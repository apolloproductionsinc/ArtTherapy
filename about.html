about

art / therapy is a creative studio and production company. Ideas, art, experiences, and everything in between.

We bring creative thinking and an artist’s sensibility together with end-to-end production, live experiences, and advisory work. Creativity is the purpose. Production and practical execution are how it gets made.

We help brands stay current with modern tools and strategies, streamline creative workflows, and strengthen their teams. We know how to integrate brands into youth culture. And we take work from the first strategic question through to delivery, building the right team around each project.
